# BT-SAL — Adversarial back-test of the saliency claims behind the periodicity pivot

**Default: NOT VERIFIED.** "Already committed" raised confidence by zero. Trusted
nothing — not the benchmark rewrite, not the V-RC4 SSA. Host-only; no firmware edit,
flash, or commit.

## What was attacked

- **#8a** — that `scripts/regression-harness/musical_saliency_benchmark.py` is
  *correct*: the `boundary_lift` metric, segments-recall, per-feature raw delta-lift,
  frame alignment, the lift window, and silence exclusion — auditing for any bug that
  could *fake a null*.
- **#8b** — RC-4 ("instantaneous saliency is structure-blind"): that boundary-lift ≈ 1.0
  not just for the three weak surrogate features but, per V-RC4, also for the *strong*
  standard structure feature (Foote self-similarity-matrix novelty). V-RC4's own
  scripts (`_scratch/v_rc4_*.py`) were deleted post-run, so its Foote numbers were
  re-derived **independently from scratch**, not trusted.

## #8a — benchmark audit (read every line of the metric path)

**No null-manufacturing bug.** Specifically checked, and clean:

- **Segment parsing** (`load_gt` `:218–229`). Reads `segments/{key}.txt`, takes
  `float(parts[0]) * 1000.0` = section-start time in ms. Verified file format is
  `time_seconds label` (e.g. `8.495568 verse`); column-0 is the boundary onset. Correct.
  The `youtube_id → file_key → BPM` join via `index.jsonl` + `metadata.csv` is sound.
- **Frame alignment** (`_boundary_lift_sums` `:287–316`). Engine OA lift consumes the
  C++ `O` rows whose `ms` is echoed verbatim from the replay input; raw-delta lift zips
  `frame_ms` with `np.diff(feature)` — aligned by construction. `bisect_left` +
  candidate `{idx-1, idx}` correctly finds the nearest boundary. No off-by-one that
  would systematically push near-frames into the far set.
- **Lift window.** Engine OA uses ±1.5 s; raw transient deltas use a tight ±250 ms with
  an explicit, *correct* rationale (a 1.5 s window dilutes a transient spike over ~400
  frames → false ≈1.0). The asymmetry is principled, not a thumb on the scale; the doc
  even runs ±1.5 s as a self-check.
- **Silence handling.** `if sil: continue` excludes silence frames from *both* near and
  far pools (`:302`), so the far-baseline isn't deflated by silence zeros — this makes
  the lift *harder* to inflate, the conservative direction. The early `audio.silence`
  gate in the engine + silence-classified event accounting (`_window_sums`) are
  consistent.
- **Lift is rate-decoupled.** `boundary_lift` is a threshold-free level probe; it does
  not read the event stream, so it cannot be gamed by the firing rate (precision-vs-
  sparse-boundaries is structurally capped at ~0.2 by a dense ~60-epm stream — correctly
  demoted to informational).

**Live reproduction (full 36 tracks):** novelty **0.9762**, spectral_energy **0.9481**,
chroma_strength **1.0314**, engine-OA boundary_lift **0.9615** — byte-exact to the
committed table and to V-RC4's calibration. recall_segments 0.124, music_epm 61.7,
silence_epm 0.0.

**Two honest caveats (neither manufactures the null; both flagged for the record):**

1. **Subset fragility.** On an 8-track subset, `spectral_energy` raw-delta lift spikes
   to **1.336** (above the 1.2 "signal exists" bar). It collapses to 0.948 at the full
   36. The full-corpus verdict is the trustworthy one, but the metric is high-variance on
   small N — any future verdict must quote the full corpus, never a `--limit` subset.
2. The benchmark's verdict is sound *as written*; it is not over-fit to produce a null.

## #8b — independent Foote-SSM re-derivation (V-RC4's deleted scripts not trusted)

New, self-contained harness left in-tree:
`scripts/regression-harness/foote_ssm_boundary_lift.py`. Shares only the corpus + the
GT loader; the MFCC front-end, mel filterbank, DCT, cosine self-similarity matrix,
Foote checkerboard-kernel novelty, recurrence novelty, boundary-lift, and
peak-pick/recall are all implemented here from scratch. Same fork framing
(12.8 kHz, HOP 96 → 133.33 Hz, NFFT 512, Hanning). Boundary-lift semantics are
**byte-identical** to the committed `_boundary_lift_sums`.

**Independent boundary-lift (pooled, full 36 tracks):**

| feature (MFCC-based) | ±250 ms | ±1.5 s | ±3 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| foote_ssm @0.5 s pool | 1.268 | 1.143 | 0.905 |
| foote_ssm @1.0 s pool | 1.278 | 1.203 | 1.143 |
| foote_ssm @2.0 s pool | 1.202 | 1.101 | 1.300 |
| mfcc_delta (instantaneous) | 1.023 | 0.980 | 0.958 |
| recurrence_novelty @1.0 s | 0.856 | 0.949 | 0.943 |

My Foote pooled-lift (1.10–1.30) sits **higher** than V-RC4's transcribed "<0.88 at
every window" — i.e. **V-RC4 understated the raw Foote lift.** Per-track medians are
higher still (Foote@1 s median **3.88**, 68 % of tracks >1.3). On the lift metric alone,
this *looks like it might refute RC-4.*

**It does not — the decisive boundary-detection test settles it.** Peak-picking the
Foote SSM novelty (the textbook way structure detectors place cuts) and recalling
HarmonixSet boundaries at a generous ±3 s:

- Foote peak boundary recall = **23/428 = 0.0537**
- Random-peak chance (same peak count) = **0.0691**
- Per-track recall median **0.072** vs per-track chance median **0.065**;
  mean (recall − chance) = **−0.015**, positive on only **15/36** tracks (worse than a
  coin-flip).

→ Foote is **AT/BELOW chance**, independently reproducing V-RC4's decisive number
(V-RC4: 0.072 vs 0.101). **Reconciliation of the 2.8× per-track lift with at-chance
recall:** the lift is a *diffuse* window-mean elevation — boundaries co-occur with
denser/louder regions where MFCC self-dissimilarity is broadly higher across the whole
±W band — **not a localised peak at the cut.** A detector that raises its window-mean
2.8× yet localises boundaries no better than random is the operational definition of
*structure-blind at this framing*. That is exactly RC-4's claim.

**Limitation (flagged, does not change the verdict):** the corpus clips are short
(~25 s), so 2 s Foote pooling yields very few self-similarity frames (P≈12) and needed a
narrowed kernel; the trustworthy resolutions are 0.5 s/1.0 s. A longer-clip corpus would
strengthen, not weaken, this test.

## Refutation attempt — did ANY faithful structure feature lift AND localise?

No. The strongest standard feature (Foote SSM) clears 1.2 on diffuse lift at one
resolution but **fails the localisation test at/below chance**; the instantaneous
timbral feature (mfcc_delta ≈ 1.0) and the recurrence feature (<1.0) do not even clear
the lift bar. No feature both lifts ≥1.3 *and* localises boundaries above chance. The
pivot was not fed weak features and then declared a null — the canonical strong feature,
implemented independently, agrees.

VERDICT (≤12 lines)

- **#8a — my benchmark CORRECT.** No bug fakes the null. Segment parse, frame alignment,
  ±250 ms/±1.5 s windows, and silence exclusion are all sound (silence exclusion biases
  *against* inflation). Committed numbers reproduce byte-exact on the full 36 tracks.
  Caveat: high variance on small subsets — `spectral_energy` hits 1.34 on 8 tracks,
  0.948 on 36; always quote the full corpus.
- **#8b — RC-4 ROBUST.** Independent Foote SSM: pooled boundary-lift 1.10–1.30 (I found
  V-RC4 *understated* the raw lift), but peak-pick boundary recall **0.0537 vs chance
  0.0691 — at/below chance**, per-track delta −0.015 (skill on only 15/36). No faithful
  structure feature both lifts ≥1.3 and localises boundaries above chance.
- **Independent Foote number vs V-RC4:** my recall **0.0537** ≈ V-RC4's **0.072** (both
  at/below their chance baselines) — agree on the verdict; my pooled lift (≤1.30) runs a
  little *higher* than V-RC4's transcribed <0.88, so if anything V-RC4 was conservative.
- **Pivot justified?** Yes, on independent evidence: instantaneous/short-frame structure
  features genuinely cannot *localise* section boundaries at the fork's 133 Hz framing
  (at/below chance), even when a feature shows diffuse near-boundary elevation. Section
  structure is a slow/periodic property — moving the musical primitive to
  periodicity/ACF is the correct response, not a premature pivot.
