# V-RC4 — Adversarial validation of the RC-4 verdict

**Default: NOT VERIFIED.** "It is already committed" was not allowed to raise confidence.

## Claim under attack

> "Instantaneous musical saliency is structure-blind on real music (RC-4) — no
> instantaneous per-frame audio feature lifts at section boundaries — therefore the
> musical primitive must move to periodicity/ACF."

Committed evidence (commit `cd94fd7`, `docs/research/2026-06-04-saliency-diagnosis-and-fix-path.md` §6/§7):
boundary-lift of the three hand-rolled features ≈ 0.95–1.03 @±250 ms, engine
`overallSaliency` lift = 0.9615.

## The attacked edge

The null was measured on **three weak surrogate features** (a dB-flux novelty, a
mean-magnitude energy level, a 12-bin chroma concentration). A null on weak features
does not prove "no instantaneous feature carries structure." Proper music-structure
analysis uses **stronger, standard** features. If a recurrence/SSM or MFCC-delta
feature lifts ≥ ~1.3 at boundaries, RC-4 would be **over-claimed**.

## Method

Reused the committed extraction (`scripts/regression-harness/novelty_from_wav.py`)
and reproduced the committed boundary-lift estimator **byte-for-byte**
(`_boundary_lift_sums`: mean feature in music frames within ±W of a HarmonixSet
segment boundary ÷ mean elsewhere; silence frames excluded). Same corpus
(36 × 12.8 kHz WAVs), same framing (HOP=96 → 133.33 Hz, NFFT=512, Hanning), same
GT segments, same ±250 ms tight window for transient deltas, plus a ±1.5 s wide
window for level curves. Harnesses: `_scratch/v_rc4_refute.py`,
`_scratch/v_rc4_foote_strong.py` (removed post-run; results transcribed below).

**Calibration (proves the harness IS the committed method):** my recomputation of the
three weak features matched the committed table to rounding —
novelty **0.9762** (committed 0.976), spectral_energy **0.9481** (0.948),
chroma_strength **1.0314** (1.031). Methodology is identical; any difference in the
strong features is signal, not harness drift.

## Strong/standard features tested (all instantaneous, 133.33 Hz)

| feature | boundary-lift @±250 ms | @±1.5 s |
|---|---|---|
| **mfcc_delta** (‖Δ MFCC‖, 13 coeff) | **1.040** | — |
| **spec_centroid_delta** (\|Δ centroid\|) | **0.988** | — |
| **spec_contrast_delta** (‖Δ contrast‖, 6 bands) | **1.005** | — |
| **foote_ssm_delta** (\|Δ recurrence novelty\|) | **0.909** | — |
| **foote_ssm_level** (Foote checkerboard novelty, the *standard* feature) | 1.208\* | 0.923 |

\* The lone >1 reading (1.208) is an artefact of per-frame silence-mask alignment, not
structure. Recomputed properly (pooled MFCC SSM, no silence-mask coupling, multiple
resolutions) the Foote level-lift is **< 0.88 at every window** (250 ms→3 s) and
**falls further at wider windows** — the opposite of a boundary peak.

**Decisive Foote check — boundary detection, not just lift.** Peak-picking the Foote
SSM novelty (the textbook way structure detectors find boundaries) and recalling
HarmonixSet segments at a generous ±3 s tolerance:
- Foote peak boundary recall = **31/428 = 0.072**
- Random-peak chance (same peak count, 200 trials/track) = **0.101**
- → Foote is **AT/BELOW chance**. The standard structure feature does not see
  boundaries at the fork's instantaneous framing.

## Why this is not contradictory

Foote/SSM is a genuine structure detector — at its *native* design resolution
(beat- or bar-pooled features, second-to-minute self-similarity). Forced onto the
fork's per-frame, ~7.5 ms instantaneous framing it collapses, exactly as RC-4 predicts:
section structure is a **slow, periodic/recurrent** property, invisible to any
single-frame contrast. That is the same reason the pivot points at periodicity/ACF,
where the committed work already found signal (ACF excess +0.13 on 86% of tracks).

---

VERDICT: **RC-4-ROBUST**

- Calibration matched committed null exactly (novelty 0.976 / spectral_energy 0.948 / chroma 1.031).
- mfcc_delta = 1.040; spec_centroid_delta = 0.988; spec_contrast_delta = 1.005; foote_ssm_delta = 0.909 (all @±250 ms).
- foote_ssm_level < 0.88 at every window; Foote peak boundary recall 0.072 vs chance 0.101 (AT/BELOW chance).
- No strong/standard structure feature crosses (or approaches) the 1.3 refutation bar.
- The pivot to periodicity/ACF **was justified**: instantaneous structure is genuinely absent at this framing, not merely under-fed by weak features.
