# BT-ACF — Adversarial back-test: V-GAP vs V-OCTAVE on the firmware ACF winner

**[HOST-ONLY — no firmware edit, no flash, no commit. Default posture: NOT VERIFIED until measured.]**

Re-runnable artefact: `scripts/regression-harness/bt_acf_4way.py` (4-way ablation +
per-track faithfulness audit + ACF-std reconciliation). Firmware anchor from
`tempo_accuracy.py` (compiles & runs the real `sb_tempo.cpp` @ HEAD `0929814`).

## The contradiction under test

- **V-GAP claimed:** the firmware-faithful ACF replica reproduces in-range **Acc2 = 40.6%**
  (better than the 28.1% Step-2 baseline); the 40.6→53 gap is the rolling-512 window.
- **V-OCTAVE claimed:** the ACF salience is **FLAT** on real novelty (std **0.02878** on
  *every* track, "monotone decay, not periodic"; peak at 210.5 BPM on all 5 x2 tracks).

These cannot both be naively true: a flat/dead ACF cannot lift accuracy from 28% to 40.6%.

## Verdict

| | Verdict | Decisive evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **V-GAP** | **RIGHT** | Replica is per-track-faithful (32/32); ACF lifts Acc2 28.1→40.6 and **beats prior-alone by +25.0pp**. |
| **V-OCTAVE** | **WRONG** (on the firmware ACF) | Firmware-band ACF salience std = 0.18–0.35 (NOT 0.029, 0/32 match); it **peaks at ~2×GT** on 3/5 x2 tracks. The "flat 0.02878" is a harness artefact on a different (non-firmware) wide-band ACF. |

V-OCTAVE's *upstream* facts (the 5 x2 tracks, det in 140–155, GT in 71–85) are correct;
its *mechanism* claim (C3b REFUTED, "ACF doesn't peak at 2×GT, root cause is Goertzel
pinning") is **wrong** — the firmware ACF genuinely peaks at the double tempo.

## (1) Faithfulness — replica reproduces the COMPILED firmware per-track

The replica drives the exact firmware winner pipeline (peak-hold /3 → 44.44 Hz;
decayed-512 ring @0.999/emit; per-emit EMA autoranger; firmware lag band; per-bin
parabolic ACF salience; `score = salience × log-Gaussian prior`; 96-bin integer argmax
+ +10%/5-tick hysteresis). Its per-track detected BPM was compared to the actual
compiled `sb_tempo.cpp` (`tempo-octave-baseline.tracks.csv`, in-range tracks):

> **per-track exact-BPM match (|Δ| ≤ 1.5 BPM): 32 / 32 (100%). 0 divergences.**

V-GAP's 40.6% is therefore **per-track faithful**, not a coincidental aggregate. The
replica is a sound instrument; conclusions drawn on it are valid for the firmware.
(`tempo_accuracy.py` re-run confirmed the anchor: in-range Acc1 28.1%, Acc2 40.6%,
octaves `{off:18, x1:13, x2:5}` = 13/32.)

## (2) The decisive 4-way ablation (same pipeline, swap only the selection signal)

| Signal (winner-selection only) | n | Acc1 | Acc2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `step2` — de-sharpened(^0.25) quartic-Goertzel-smooth × prior (PRE-ACF Step-2) | 32 | 21.9% | 25.0% |
| `acf` — ACF-salience × prior (**THE COMMIT — `sb_sel_score`**) | 32 | **28.1%** | **40.6%** |
| `acf_only` — ACF-salience ALONE (no prior) | 32 | 25.0% | 40.6% |
| `prior_only` — prior ALONE (constant tactus weight) | 32 | 15.6% | 15.6% |

**Deltas:**

- `acf − prior_only` = **+12.5 pp Acc1, +25.0 pp Acc2**. Prior-alone is *not* doing the work:
  with no ACF the detector collapses to 15.6%. **The ACF salience is the load-bearing signal.**
- `acf − step2` = **+6.2 pp Acc1, +15.6 pp Acc2**. The commit beats the signal it replaced.
- `acf − acf_only` = **+3.1 pp Acc1, +0.0 pp Acc2** — the prior is mildly net-positive (it
  flips 10 tracks' winners, net +1 Acc1) but not the source of the gain. V-GAP's
  "−prior = +0.0 pp" is correct *for Acc2*; it understated the small +3.1 pp Acc1 lift.

**Adversarial hypothesis — "ACF is net-useless-or-harmful vs prior-alone" — is REFUTED.**
The ACF commit is a real, large accuracy gain (+25 pp Acc2 over prior-alone, +15.6 pp over
the signal it replaced), not a regression for no gain.

## (3) ACF-salience variation — reconciling the std=0.02878 claim

Measured on the **firmware winner band** (the 96 bins 60–155 BPM the argmax actually sees),
settled emit, per in-range track:

> std: **min 0.18, mean 0.28, max 0.35**. spread (max−mean): mean 0.72.
> tracks with std == 0.02878 (±1e-4): **0 / 32.**

The firmware ACF is **strongly peaked, not flat**. V-OCTAVE's 0.02878 is **unreproducible**:

- It is not the firmware ACF (firmware lag band = 15–50 = 55–160 BPM; V-OCTAVE reported
  "lag 38–145 = 55–210 BPM" and "peak 210.5" — a *different, wider* ACF the winner never sees).
- Even a faithful **whole-clip wide-band** ACF (`np.correlate`, 55–210 BPM, the
  `ac_ceiling_bpm` style V-OCTAVE appears to have used) gives std **0.18–0.26** and a
  *variable* peak (140/151/210/200 BPM across tracks), **not** an identical 0.02878 / 210.5.
- An identical-to-5-decimal-places std and an identical peak BPM across every distinct track
  is the signature of a **measurement-harness artefact** (e.g. the same buffer measured
  repeatedly, or an un-normalised/mean-subtracted constant window), not a property of real
  novelty.

### The octave mechanism — C3b is CORRECT, V-OCTAVE refuted it wrongly

Firmware-band ACF salience at the settled emit, for the 5 x2 tracks:

| Track | GT | det | sal-peak BPM | sal@GT | sal@2×GT | std | peak vs 2×GT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vDWhfsQHq1o | 71 | 140 | 140 | 0.688 | 0.947 | 0.322 | **peak ≈ 2×GT** |
| ekAXPCphKXQ | 74 | 148 | 148 | 0.606 | 1.000 | 0.325 | **peak ≈ 2×GT** |
| SOPdS8jGaeI | 75 | 152 | 152 | 0.120 | 0.990 | 0.306 | **peak ≈ 2×GT** |
| WBKnpyoFEBo | 80 | 155 | 148 | 0.329 | (2×=160, OOR) | 0.316 | peak at band-top (2×GT out of range) |
| MYRNGeIJMiQ | 85 | 155 | 155 | 0.067 | (2×=170, OOR) | 0.264 | peak at band-top (2×GT out of range) |

On the 3 tracks whose double is representable, the ACF salience **genuinely peaks at the
double tempo** (sal@2×GT 0.95–1.0 vs sal@GT 0.07–0.69) — a true harmonic-multiple
ambiguity. On the other 2, 2×GT (160, 170) exceeds the 155-BPM ceiling, so the peak pins
at the top bin. This is **exactly C3b** ("x2 errors caused by ACF harmonic-multiple
ambiguity"), which V-OCTAVE labelled OTHER-CAUSE. V-OCTAVE's "Goertzel-pinning" root cause
is mis-attributed: the live winner signal is the ACF, and the ACF itself prefers the octave.

## Implications for the commit

- **The ACF commit (0929814) is a real gain**, not neutral and not a regression-for-no-gain.
  It is the dominant lever taking in-range Acc2 from 25.0% (Step-2) to 40.6%.
- Its **one genuine liability** is the 2×-tempo octave ambiguity that V-OCTAVE *correctly
  observed at the track level but mis-diagnosed*. That is a **separable octave-defence
  problem** (the ACF prefers the double), not evidence the ACF signal should be reverted.
  Note this is a *different* problem from the half/double-arbiter V-OCTAVE tested (C3c):
  that arbiter was scored on a flat-ACF assumption that is itself wrong, so C3c's "DOESNT"
  verdict should be re-derived against the *actual* peaked ACF before it is trusted.
- V-GAP's window finding (40.6 → 53 is the rolling-512 window) is **untouched and remains
  the right lever** for closing the gap to the host ceiling; this back-test only adds that
  the signal being windowed is real and load-bearing.

## Caveats / scope

- 32 in-range HarmonixSet tracks (60–155 BPM); ±1 track = ±3.1 pp. The +25.0 pp ACF-over-
  prior Acc2 delta (8 tracks) and the +15.6 pp ACF-over-Step-2 (5 tracks) are far outside noise.
- Host clean novelty (`novelty_from_wav`); on-device sb_tempo consumes worse AGC-clamped
  GDFT novelty, so 40.6% is an optimistic firmware bound. All comparisons here are
  same-novelty relative, so the verdicts are unaffected by that absolute caveat.
- `step2` is a faithful but not bit-exact replica of the pre-ACF Goertzel branch (it omits
  inter-bin phase bookkeeping irrelevant to magnitude selection); it is the comparator, not
  the anchor. The anchor (`acf`) is validated 32/32 against the compiled firmware.
