## What is wrong

`scripts/generate_legacy_audio_vectors.py` produces its 24 kHz copies with a blind string substitution:

```python
text.replace('12800', '24000').replace('12800.0', '24000.0').replace('96', '180')
```

`'96'` is replaced **anywhere it appears as a substring**, including in numbers and array bounds that have nothing to do with the hop. Verified against the scratch copies on disk:

| `oracle_tempo.py` | 2 (real) | `float sm[96]` → `sm[180]`, and `k1_tempo_debug_dump(sm, 96, …)` → `(sm, 180, …)` — **that 96 is the tempo-bin count, not a hop** | Rate did change, but the tracker is asked to dump 180 bins when it has 96 |
| `oracle_onset_beat.py` | **0** | two fixture loop bounds `for (int i = 0; i < 96; i++)` — the hi-hat train length | **No rate change at all.** The stimulus was lengthened. Records went 319 → 487 |

Consequences, all confirmed by comparing bytes:

- `tempo_24k180.golden.jsonl` is **byte-identical** to the frozen 12.8 kHz `tempo.golden.jsonl`.

## Emotiscope — the golden is empty

`test/fixtures/emotiscope_novelty_input.txt` is 32 bytes: sixteen zeros. The manifest says so honestly (`"16 zero novelty ticks"`), but the output is worthless as an oracle — all 96 magnitudes zero, all phases identical, on every tick.

`tempo_confidence = 10` on every record is not a measurement. In `tempo.h`, `tempi_power_sum` is floored at `1e-8` and `max_contribution` at `1e-7`, so `confidence = 1e-7 / 1e-8 = 10.0` exactly whenever there is no tempo energy. **Record this as the third Emotiscope defect** (after the no-op `unwrap_phase()` and the `tempo.h:165` out-of-bounds read): `tempo_confidence` is unbounded and reports its *maximum* value precisely when there is no signal. K1's own confidence field must be clamped and defined on silence; do not copy this shape.

To make the oracle mean something:

- The novelty history is 1024 samples at 50 Hz ≈ 20.5 s. Fewer than 1024 ticks integrates mostly zeros. Drive it with **at least 1024 ticks**, and prefer 2048 so the first window is fully populated.
- Use a **synthetic periodic novelty train at a known BPM** (say 120), generated at 50 Hz. Then the golden carries a falsifiable assertion: the peak magnitude bin must be `BPM − 60`. Assert it in the test.
- Record the clock mismatch: Emotiscope's novelty clock is 50 Hz (`NOVELTY_LOG_HZ`); K1's is `12800/96/3 = 44.44 Hz`. Feeding K1-derived novelty into this oracle mislabels the tempo axis by `50/44.44 = 1.125` — a 12.5 % BPM error. Generating the train at 50 Hz avoids it.
