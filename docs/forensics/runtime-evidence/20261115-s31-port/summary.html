What works

  • The FE firmware builds for the ESP32-S31 on plain ESP-IDF 6.1.1, from the same 78 files and the same compiler settings as the S3 build. Arduino 4.0 RC1 and FastLED 3.10.3 come in as ESP-IDF components.
  • The S31 image starts in the emulator and prints its build line. All 28 replay cases are accepted.
  • 27 of 28 cases give the same light frames as the S3, bit for bit, on both edges. That includes both real-music cases (1,334 frames each).
  • The S3 builds (k1_fe and Main RPL) are unchanged, byte for byte, in the sections the repo's check compares.

What differs

  • Only the startup case. Its clock starts at the emulated boot time, and the emulator boots the S31 image 200 ms later than the S3 image (546 ms against 346 ms). With the clock fixed at 1.0 s, as in the other music cases, S3 and S31 are identical (1,067 frames).
  • The ordered board (Gx2 black-gold-v6) does not connect line-in data, I2C, the USB-C current sense, two user buttons or the heartbeat LED to the S31.

What was set or switched off

  • The two LED buffer enables are pulled low on Gx2, so the S31 firmware drives them high at start.
  • The signals the board does not wire are marked "not connected", and the firmware leaves them alone. Line-in, the buttons and USB-C power qualification cannot work until wires are added. LED power stays at the USB default limit.
  • The USB update-mode callback is left out on the S31, because this Arduino core has no TinyUSB. The FE does not use that mode.
  • No driver had to be stubbed: ESP-IDF 6.1.1 has every driver the FE uses. No Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

Not tested (needs a real S31-Zero)

  • Anything running on silicon: microphone on I2S0 (slot order and level), the four LED lines on the S31's four RMT channels, USB serial, LittleFS, PSRAM, timing and core load.
  • LED wire order within an edge (no LED bar exists yet), the Zero variant (N16R16 assumed), and the 3.3 V level of GPIO20–25.
  • The LED waveform: the emulator's RMT loopback needs a receive channel the K1 does not have.

First three things when boards arrive

  1. Plug the S31-Zero into Gx2, flash the S31 k1_fe image over its USB-C, and check the boot and the :build reply.
  2. Microphone: record the IM69D through I2S0 and confirm slot order and level against the S3.
  3. LEDs: check all four lines with the enables high, then set the DIN-A/DIN-B order on a real bar.
