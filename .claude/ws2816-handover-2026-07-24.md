## KEY FACTS / TRAPS
- All WS2816 work is bench-only; WS2816 is NOT a ratified production part. `k1_hardware`/`k1_prod_im73d` stay byte-identical.
- Never run two `pio run` concurrently (SCons `.sconsign` race → spurious `.d`-file failures). pytest (host g++) does NOT race with one pio build.
- The research crux (verified): WS2816's 16-bit depth is **unrealised in the stock FastLED path**; its real wins are low-light banding + 10 kHz PWM camera-flicker, NOT brightness/gamut.
- LED_FPS ~200 on the split (2× the 100 FPS target); the wire is 48 bits/pixel whether packed 8- or 16-bit, so depth is "free at the wire."
