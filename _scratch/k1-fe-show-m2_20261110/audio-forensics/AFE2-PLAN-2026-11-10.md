## 1. Objective and measure of success

The K1 looks its best on any music at any room volume, unattended. In the Captain's Light-Show Law: the light hits when the music hits (1, 2), it is never idle while music plays (4), and loud and quiet look different so a drop looks like a drop (7).

Numbers, measured on LED output in the emulator and on the bench unit, on at least seven tracks of different kinds at three room levels:
- **Section tracking**: correlation between 3 s average brightness and 3 s loudness. Today (level signal) −0.54 to +0.79.
- **Contrast**: brightness in the loudest quarter of a track divided by the quietest quarter. Today 0.95 to 1.53 (level signal); 0.45 to 0.72 chorus-to-quiet on LED output for Loreen.
- **Beat movement kept**: at least 85 % of today's frame-to-frame movement.
- **Room invariance**: the same result at −10, 0 and +10 dB playback.
- **No change when off**: with a stage switched off, output is byte-identical to the build before it.
Then the Captain's four questions on the plate: does it hit when the music hits; does it breathe; is it ever idle; can I follow one thing.

## 2. What is wrong today (evidence)

Full analysis: `research_notes/K1 audio front end design/system_first_principles.md`; report: `reports/K1 audio front end design.md`; source map: `source/CHAIN-MAP.md`; history: `history/HISTORY-LEDGER.md`.

- Thirteen stages touch level; four normalise by themselves (follower 2 s, four-band AGC 0.5 s, chroma 10 s, guard input trim), three are gates, one is a guard with five actions.
- All of them act faster than a musical section. No effect is told how loud the music is compared with the last minute.
- The fixed x21.6 gain exists only because the Goertzel discards the bottom 6 bits (`audio/k1_gdft_lane4_exact.h:24`). It is the only thing that can clip (the microphone's own ceiling is about 130 dB SPL, datasheet).
- The loud guard's trigger reads the self-normalised level, so it fires on dense music at any volume (active 89-97 % of music lines at room volume, down to 34 %).
- The four-band AGC gives quiet passages about 20x the bass gain of loud ones (0.086 against 1.5-2.0, bench telemetry).
- "Silence" latches for 9 s in loud dense music and resets beat tracking.
- About fourteen level-control patches in five months; none removed. Archetype: fixes that fail, shifting the burden.
- Upstream Sensory Bridge (1.0.0-4.1.1) has no multi-band AGC and no loud guard; these come from our fork line.

## 3. Design: measure once, "what x how much", one slow exposure

1. **One meter** on the raw hop (before gain and clamp), high-passed at 110 Hz: section loudness `S` (30 ms up, 1.5 s down).
2. **One anchor** `A`, the only automatic gain reference in the system: rises in 3 s, falls over 60 s; if the music stays more than 6 dB under it for 45 s it re-anchors in about 4 s. Never below a fixed minimum.
3. **how much** = clamp((S / A)^(gamma / 2), floor, 1). gamma 1: proportional to sound pressure. gamma 0: exactly today.
4. **what** = today's fast, level-free signals, unchanged: the 2 s beat envelope, onsets, beat phase, pitch class.
5. Everything the effects receive becomes `what x how much`, from the same anchor: the level path first, then the spectrum, then VU.
6. Silence is one test on the same meter. It never zeroes a live signal.
7. Removed by the end: loud guard, four-band AGC, fixed gain and clamp, the crest-based silence logic, the calibration ritual.

Specification: `design/afe2_reference.py`. Firmware: `audio/k1_afe2.h`, `audio/k1_afe2.cpp`. Starting parameters and their sources are in the reference's header. gamma, floor and the three anchor times are the parameters for the Captain's eye; the rest follow from the research.

### Decision analysis (why this and not the alternatives)

MUSTs: (M1) nothing clips at any real venue level; (M2) unattended from quiet room to loud venue, no ritual; (M3) today's beat punch kept; (M4) loud and quiet sections look different; (M5) never a gap while music plays; (M6) no added latency; (M7) every stage added removes one; (M8) one-line revert and byte-identical when off; (M9) Core 0 real-time safe.

| Alternative | M1 | M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M7 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Retarget the guard only | no | no | yes | no | yes | no | fails M1, M2, M4 |
| B. Keep the chain, add "how much" on the level path, fix silence, drop the guard's false trigger | no | partly | yes | waveform modes only | yes | partly | the first three stages of D |
| C. Port Emotiscope's front end | yes | yes | yes | **no** (built to look the same at any level) | yes | yes | fails M4 |
| D. AFE2: one meter, one anchor, for level, spectrum and VU | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | **chosen** |

B is not a rival to D: it is D's opening moves. So the order below is B first (small, visible, reversible), then the structural stages.

### Simplicity checks (what was left out on purpose)

- No dB SPL calibration: the exposure is relative, so absolute units are not needed.
- No separate silence gate in the exposure: the 45 s rule and the anchor minimum cover it.
- No new signal names for effects in stage 1: the published level is multiplied in one place, so the 9 effect files that read it need no edit.
- No per-band anything.

## 4. Stages

Each stage: its own runtime switch where the hardware allows, default off until accepted, byte-identical when off, one objective A/B in the emulator, one bench A/B on the Main RPL, then the Captain's eye. Repair authority for every stage: the owner fixes and re-runs without asking. A stage is not done until it is built, flashed to the Main RPL and observed.

### AFE2-S1 — Exposure on the level path
- **Purpose.** Modes that read `waveform_peak_scaled` / `peak_scaled` (modes 22, 32 and the waveform family, 9 effect files) get section dynamics: `published = beat envelope x how_much`.
- **Inputs.** Raw IM69D hop `im69d_samples_i16`; reference `design/afe2_reference.py`; base `a6bad318`.
- **Outputs.** `audio/k1_afe2.{h,cpp}`; edits in `audio/i2s_audio.h` (meter call, envelope on its own state `k1_afe2_peak_env`, guard trigger reads the envelope, telemetry `afe= how= env= sec= anc= fast=`), `system/system.h` (calibration reset), `serial/` (commands `:k1_afe=on|off|status`, `:k1_afe_gamma=0..2`, `:k1_afe_floor=0..1`, RAM only), `platformio.ini` (bench env default on), `tests/test_k1_afe2_native.py`, `tests/k1_afe2_host.cpp`. Branch `fe/k1-afe2`, parent `a6bad318`.
- **Acceptance.** (a) Host: firmware module equals the reference on every hop of a 100 s programme within 0.002, and the named behaviours hold (done: 3 passed). (b) Emulator, exposure off: frames byte-identical to the `a6bad318` captures `emu/runs/tail/tail-m{22,32,18}-on`. (c) Emulator, exposure on: the published level equals `model envelope x reference how_much` on the same samples; brightness in the final chorus divided by the breakdown rises; movement kept at least 85 %. (d) Commit gate: `pytest tests/` and `pio run -e k1_hardware` in the export; `k1_main_rpl_show_bench`, `k1_main_rpl_im69d`, `k1_fe` build. (e) Prepared-show gates G1-G4 unchanged on the commit. (f) Bench: flashed to the Main RPL by the handover section 10 method; `:k1_afe=status` answers; one passage captured with the exposure off and on. (g) Captain's four questions.
- **Failure.** (b) not identical: find the stray difference before anything else; nothing ships with an unexplained change. (c) level does not match: the meter is not seeing the samples the model assumes; fix the tap. Captain says quiet sections look dead: raise floor, lower gamma (runtime), then re-ask.
- **STOP.** Device identity mismatch; a calibration need; G1-G4 regress.

### AFE2-S2 — One silence test, from the same meter
- **Purpose.** Stop "silence" latching in loud dense music (9 s measured) and resetting beat and onset tracking. Silence becomes: section loudness under a fixed floor for the dwell time. No crest term.
- **Inputs.** `audio/i2s_audio.h:1392-1471` (classifier), consumers `audio/k1_onset_beat.cpp:257,547`, directors, STM; bench telemetry of quiet-room and music levels; S1's meter.
- **Outputs.** The IM69D silence branch replaced under a switch; the floor stated in raw counts with its measurement; the SSL-fraction and crest thresholds for this microphone deleted when accepted.
- **Acceptance.** Emulator: no silence flag during any music passage of the seven-track set at −10, 0, +10 dB; silence declared within the dwell on true digital silence and on a recorded quiet room. Bench: zero `silence=1` lines during a full track at the Captain's volume; `silence=1` within the dwell after playback stops. Beat confidence during dense choruses does not collapse.
- **Failure.** Quiet-room noise above the floor: measure the high-passed quiet-room level on the unit (needs only a quiet moment, not a calibration command) and set the floor from it.
- **STOP.** Any need to run `start_noise_cal`.

### AFE2-S3 — Loud guard: remove the false trigger and the spectrum-side actions
- **Purpose.** The guard stops cutting the picture at ordinary volume.
- **Inputs.** `audio/i2s_audio.h:229-282`, `audio/k1_gdft_core.cpp:505-516, 598-606`, colour unpin in `visual/lightshow_modes.h`; emulator results `emu/analysis/matrix.md`, `emu/analysis-tail/tail.md`.
- **Outputs.** `peak_pin` and `spec_sat` no longer set `gdft_trim`; target, floor, floor-cut, ceiling knee and colour unpin code deleted; the input trim (real near-clip protection) stays until S5. `tests/test_k1_loud_guard_static.py` updated in the same commit.
- **Acceptance.** Emulator at room level: equals today's "guard off" captures. Emulator at the hot fixture level: equals today's "guard on" behaviour for the input side (no hard clipping). Bench: `gdft_trim` absent or 1.000 throughout a track.
- **Failure.** Mode 32 looks dimmer without the guard (measured −33 % at room level): judge after S1 and S4 are in, since both change mode 32's level; do not re-add the guard to brighten a mode.
- **STOP.** None specific.

### AFE2-S4 — Spectrum exposure: one gain replaces the four-band AGC
- **Purpose.** The spectrum the effects read keeps its balance and its section dynamics: every bin is divided by the same slow anchor, with a fixed tilt and a fixed soft ceiling. Quiet passages no longer get 20x the gain of loud ones.
- **Inputs.** `audio/k1_gdft_core.cpp:464-611` (AGC, tilt, knee, clamp); S1's anchor; the 21 June "louder looks dimmer" record (a fast single gain; this is a slow one).
- **Outputs.** New scaling path under a switch; `K1_AGC_PERBAND_V1` code and the broadband `#else` deleted when accepted; telemetry `agc_env` (dead) removed.
- **Acceptance.** Emulator, seven tracks, three levels, spectral modes: section tracking and contrast up, movement kept, no band stuck at zero or at the ceiling; colour path (palette vibrancy) unchanged or better by hue-bin count. Bench A/B. Captain's eye on at least modes 22, 32 and two spectral modes.
- **Failure.** Bass dominates the picture: adjust the fixed tilt (a constant), never add a per-band loop.
- **STOP.** If two attempts do not beat today's numbers, stop patching and redesign the mapping from the research notes (log spectrum against a fixed reference).

### AFE2-S5 — Capture at full precision; delete the guard
- **Purpose.** Nothing in the firmware can clip below the microphone's own ceiling. The fixed x21.6, the 16-bit clamp, the `>> 6` and the remaining guard go.
- **Inputs.** `audio/i2s_audio.h:981-998`, `audio/k1_gdft_lane4_exact.h:24`, `audio/k1_gdft_core.cpp:146-230`; golden spectrum tests.
- **Outputs.** Scaling carried inside the 64-bit Goertzel recurrence; identical magnitudes at room level by construction; guard code, commands and telemetry deleted; goldens regenerated with the reason recorded.
- **Acceptance.** Emulator room level: output equal to before within rounding (stated bound). Emulator at the hot fixture and 20 dB above it: no clipping, no limit cycle, picture still follows the music. Core 0 hop time not worse (measured in the emulator's cycle count or on the bench).
- **Failure.** Timing cost: keep the shift but move the gain inside the recurrence at reduced precision.
- **STOP.** Any Core 0 overrun.

### AFE2-S6 — VU from the meter
- **Purpose.** The 11 effect files that read `vu_level` get a level that is exposed to the room (today it is an absolute RMS, about 0.05 at room volume) and carries section dynamics.
- **Inputs.** `calculate_vu()` (`audio/i2s_audio.h:1873`), S1's meter and anchor.
- **Outputs.** VU = fast level against the anchor, through the same curve; `VU_LEVEL_FLOOR` calibration use removed.
- **Acceptance.** Emulator: VU modes at −10, 0, +10 dB look the same after the anchor settles; contrast up. Bench A/B. Captain's eye on mode 18.
- **STOP.** None specific.

### AFE2-S7 — Line-in, ritual and dead code
- **Purpose.** The FE board's line input gets the same meter and anchor (`audio/k1_audio_ingress.h`); the noise-calibration ritual is no longer needed for level or silence; the leftovers are deleted.
- **Outputs.** Meter on the line path; calibration commands reduced to what DC removal still needs or removed; dead fields and stale comments gone; `docs/hardware/device-build-registry.md` and the lane documents updated.
- **Acceptance.** FE board (when on USB): line and microphone both pass S1-S6 numbers; a unit with erased settings behaves correctly with no calibration.
- **STOP.** FE board identity not confirmed.

### AFE2-S8 — Mode 22's dark stretches (not a front-end fault)
- **Purpose.** Mode 22 draws only on the positive side of its own oscillator, so it can show nothing for seconds in a loud chorus (measured: 3 s dark at level 0.82; guard off and chroma gate off both leave it dark).
- **Outputs.** A proposal shown to the Captain as a picture before any change, because Snapwave is his reference look.
- **STOP.** No change to mode 22's look without the Captain seeing the before and after.
