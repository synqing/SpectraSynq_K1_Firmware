How to flash

Normal
  1. Plug in over USB.
  2. Connect, pick the port.
  3. S3 USB usually finds the bootloader.
  4. Quit pio device monitor if the port is held.
If sync fails
  1. Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.
  2. Retries for 20 seconds.
  3. After flashing, the board resets; press RESET if it does not.
Erase
  • Full chip erase before write.
  • Wipes NVS — config and calibration go.
  • Only for boot-loops or OTA slot errors.
Seed OTA-1
  • Writes the app to the second slot.
  • A failed later OTA still rolls back.
  • Every image is MD5-checked before and after write.
