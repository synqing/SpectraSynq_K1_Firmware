## First / second / third order

1. Pick a pretty tile.
2. That tile sits under Inspect sky, 4px grain (or not), two opaque wells, gold as identifier only.
3. Line cages (A/B/C) compete with well rims. Dense dots (E) and mesh (C) become grey noise at distance. Sparse CAD (D) reads as workspace and then disappears.

If everyone shipped C Mesh: the page would look like a texture demo. Serial type would fight the field.

## What not to do

- Fade the floor at the viewport edge (that is still an edge).
- Gold-fill the grid (identifier, not wash).
- Port until Captain says the letter. Recommendation is **D**. Alternative if they want the 80px room taught as lines: **A**.
